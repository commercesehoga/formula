Frequently Asked Questions

What is Formula Story Mode?
Formula Story Mode is an AI tool that converts any scientific formula or concept into a memorable story, real-life analogy, or memory trick — helping students retain information far better than rote memorization.
Which subjects does it support?
It works for any subject — Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology, Economics, Accountancy, or even historical facts. Just type your topic and the AI adapts its explanation style accordingly.
Can I save the generated story?
Yes. Click "Save as PDF" to download a beautifully formatted PDF of any story. Your history is also saved locally so you can revisit past generations.
Is this tool free?
Completely free. This is part of ThunderStudy's mission to provide world-class study tools to every student, at no cost.
